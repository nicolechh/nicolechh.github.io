About

Colophon

I'm Nicole Chou, a product designer working on tools people use every day for work. Before design I spent three years in technical documentation, which is where I learned that most interface problems are really naming problems wearing a costume.

I work best in the messy middle of a project — after the strategy deck and before the polish — mapping states, pressure-testing flows, and writing the words on the buttons. Most of my recent work has been in logistics, marine, and commodities software.
